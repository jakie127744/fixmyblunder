What the app is

FixMyBlunder is a free training tool. It reads public games from Lichess and Chess.com, or a PGN you paste, finds mistakes you repeat, and gives you drills for them. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lichess, Chess.com or the Stockfish project.

Using it

  • Use it for your own training. You are responsible for the username you enter and for following the rules of the sites you import from, including their limits on how often you request games.
  • Do not try to break the app, overload the sites it talks to, or use it to harass anyone.

The analysis can be wrong

The engine runs briefly on a phone, so its judgments are quick and shallow by design. Pattern names are rules of thumb, not proof. Treat results as suggestions for practice, not as a rating or a guarantee that you will improve.
